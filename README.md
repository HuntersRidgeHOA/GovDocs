# Hunter's Ridge Maintenance Association (HRMA) — Governing Documents

Searchable, verbatim transcriptions of HRMA's governing documents, made from old scanned and microfilmed copies of the originals.

> **The recorded originals are the authoritative text.** These transcriptions are for convenience — reading, searching, and quoting. If a transcription and an original ever disagree, the original controls.

## How to read the transcriptions

The text is **verbatim**: original wording, spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and typos are kept exactly as printed (for example "won proper handwriting", "23,930 acres"). Nothing has been corrected or modernized.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[?]` | Uncertain reading — the best reading of a degraded spot |
| `[illegible]` | Unreadable in the scan (sometimes with an approximate word count) |
| `{…}` | Text that is unreadable or cut off in this copy, restored from the identical survey exhibit attached to the other Declaration |
| `[handwritten: …]` | Handwritten text, including fill-ins on blank lines |
| `[signature]`, `[initials]` | A signature or initials (names are not guessed) |
| `[stamp: …]`, `[seal: …]` | Recorder, filing, or notary stamps and seals |
| **Bold** | Underlined or bold captions in the original |

Each transcription starts with a short header and ends with **Transcription Notes**. The notes list, by original page number, every remaining uncertain or unreadable spot and every quirk of the original that might look like a transcription error (typos, inconsistent spellings, missing words, blanks never filled in).

In the Markdown files, hidden comments such as `<!-- Page 5 of 20 | BOOK 819 PAGE 334 -->` mark where each original page begins, including the recorder's book and page. They don't show in the Word or PDF versions.

## How the transcriptions were made

Transcribed with AI (Claude), October 2026:

1. **Transcription:** each scanned page was transcribed twice, independently, from high-magnification views of the scan.
2. **Reconciliation:** every disagreement between the two transcriptions was settled against the image.
3. **Proofreading:** every page was proofread, with every number, date, name, and book/page reference checked character by character.
4. **Survey cross-check:** both Declarations attach the same metes-and-bounds survey, so the two copies were checked against each other.
5. **Human review:** the flagged spots were reviewed against the scanned originals and the confirmed readings were applied. The `[?]` and `[illegible]` marks that remain are spots the reviewer could not read either; their notes stay so readers know where the scans fall short.

The poorest scans are the two Declarations and the Easement, which are 144 dpi microfilm copies. The left edge of some survey pages is cut off.

## Making changes

Edit the Markdown file in `MD/` (it is the master copy), then regenerate the Word and PDF versions from it so all three stay identical.
