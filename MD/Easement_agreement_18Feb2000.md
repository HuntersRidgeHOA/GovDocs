THIS MAINTENANCE EASEMENT AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this [handwritten: 18th] day of [handwritten: February], 2000, by and between HUNTER'S RIDGE MAINTENANCE ASSOCIATION, a Delaware corporation ("HRMA"), and PAUL SCHLOSSER, of New Castle County, Delaware ("Schlosser").
