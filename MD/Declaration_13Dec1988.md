# DECLARATION

**This Declaration,** Made this [handwritten: 13TH] day of [handwritten: DECEMBER], 1988 by PAUL L. SCHLOSSER and DOROTHY J. SCHLOSSER, of New Castle County and State of Delaware ("Owner") and GRANVILLE DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION, a corporation of the State of Delaware, ("Equitable Owner"), Owner and Equitable Owner hereinafter referred to as "Declarant".

### WITNESSETH:

**Whereas,** Declarant are, respectively, the Owner and the Equitable Owner of all that certain lot, piece or parcel of land, situate in Mill Creek Hundred, New Castle County and State of Delaware, as shown on the Record Major Subdivision Plan of Hunter's Ridge (the "Plan of Hunter's Ridge"), prepared by Franco R. Bellafante, Inc.; recorded __________ in the Office of the Recorder of Deeds in and for New Castle County, State of Delaware, in Microfilm No. __________ containing 47.71± acres of land, which said tract of land is described by metes and bounds in Exhibit "A" attached hereto and made a part hereof, and which said tract is hereinafter referred to as SUBJECT LAND; and

**Whereas,** Declarant, in connection with a recent application to develop the SUBJECT LAND as a residential development, under the lot averaging option, and desires to restrict SUBJECT LAND; and

**Whereas,** Declarant desires to comply with the subdivision regulations of New Castle County with respect to providing for the maintenance of certain private open spaces, traffic islands and common parking spaces;

**Now, Therefore, Know All Men By These Presents,** That Declarant does hereby covenant and declare that it shall hold and stand seized of SUBJECT LAND, described by metes and bounds in Exhibit "A", under and subject nevertheless to the following restrictive covenants, which it is hereby agreed shall be covenants running with the land and which shall be binding upon the Declarant, its successors and assigns, and only upon SUBJECT <!-- Page 2 of 14 | printed page -2- | BOOK 815 PAGE 306 --> LAND, which said restrictive covenants are hereby imposed for the benefit of New Castle County, a political subdivision of the State of Delaware, its successors and assigns:

1\. The number of dwelling units which may be erected on SUBJECT LAND shall not exceed seventy-two (72).

2\. No dwelling unit other than a single family detached house shall be constructed upon SUBJECT LAND.

3\. Buildings to be used for recreational, security or maintenance purposes may be erected and maintained in locations within the "private open space" shown on the Plan of Hunter's Ridge, hereinafter referred to as Private Open Space, approved by said Declarant, its successors and assigns, provided the design of such buildings be approved by Declarant, its successors or assigns.

4\. Prior to the commencement of construction of dwelling units, a storm water management plan shall be approved by the New Castle County Department of Public Works.

5\. Except for the installation of utilities, including sanitary sewer and storm drainage lines, underground gas mains, water mains, water lines, and electrical cables, telephone and cable television lines, and including water facilities, consisting of a well or wells, to be located within fifteen feet (15') of the site of the present wellhead, and related above-ground facilities, not to exceed one-tenth (1/10) of one (1) acre in the aggregate, to be serviced by temporary easement only, and the construction of roads, the existing trees and other vegetation within the Private Open Space shall remain undisturbed. Any portion of the Private Open Space disturbed as a result of the installation of utilities and road construction shall be restored to its original condition consistent with good engineering practices.

6\. In order that the areas designated as Private Open Space and the areas shown on the Plan of Hunter's Ridge as traffic islands, including the common parking spaces appurtenant thereto, hereinafter referred to as the "Traffic Islands", shall be maintained, there shall be organized a corporation, hereinafter referred to as a "maintenance association", whose members shall be record owners of all lots shown on said plan. With respect to such corporation:

(a) The purchaser of any lot of land by the acceptance of a deed to said land, obligates and binds himself, <!-- Page 3 of 14 | printed page -3- | BOOK 815 PAGE 307 --> his heirs and assigns, to become a member of the maintenance association, and to be bound by all of its rules and regulations, and to be subject to all of the duties and obligations imposed by membership in said association.

(b) Each owner of any lot, by acceptance of a deed therefor, is deemed to covenant and agree to pay to the maintenance association (1) annual assessments or charges, and (2) special assessments for capital improvements, such assessments to be fixed, established, and collected from time to time as hereinafter provided, provided that all assessments must be fixed at a uniform rate for all lots. Each such assessment, together with such interest, costs and reasonable attorney's fees shall be a lien upon such real property. The assessments levied by the maintenance association shall be used for the purpose of the improvement and maintenance of the Private Open Space and Traffic Islands, together with any improvements erected thereon, now or hereafter, including, but not limited to storage for maintenance and recreational tools, equipment and supplies; guard or gate house, dam or dams, bridges, courts, or paying fields; and for maintenance of security and/or maintenance personnel to the extent that such members of the association or the majority of them shall deem necessary or desirable from time to time, including, but not limited to salaries; provided further that nothing herein contained shall be construed as prohibiting the removal of any recreational, maintenance or security structures or improvements if the majority of the members of the maintenance association deem removal of any or all of same to be desirable at any time. Said assessment shall be in sufficient amount to pay the costs of keeping the Private Open Spaces and Traffic Islands and improvements thereon in good usable condition, and to pay all expenses arising from time to time in connection with the ownership of the Private Open Spaces and Traffic Islands, including, without limitation, real property taxes and insurance, to the extent that such members of the Association, or a majority of them shall deem necessary or desirable from time to time to procure insurance coverage with respect to such ownership, and to offset any uncollected prior assessments.

(c) The amount of such assessment shall be fixed annually by the board of directors of the maintenance association, and shall be charged or assessed in equal proportion against each lot regardless of size.

(d) Any assessments which are not paid when due shall be delinquent. If the assessment is not paid within thirty (30) days after the due date, the assessment shall bear interest from the date of delinquency at the legal rate of interest under <!-- Page 4 of 14 | printed page -4- | BOOK 815 PAGE 308 --> Delaware law, and the maintenance association may bring an action at law against the owner personally obligated to pay the same, or foreclose the lien against the property, and the interest, costs, and reasonable attorney's fees of any such action shall be added to the amount of such assessment. No owner may waive or otherwise escape liability for the assessments provided for herein by non-use of the said common areas or abandonment of his lot.

(e) It is expressly agreed that the assessments referred to above shall be a lien or encumbrance on the land in respect to which said assessment are made and it is expressly stated that by acceptance of title to any of the land included in said tract the owner (not including mortgagee) from the time of acquiring title thereto shall be held to have covenanted and agreed to pay said assessments to the maintenance association.

(f) By his acceptance of title, each owner shall be held to vest in the maintenance association the right and power in his own name, to take and prosecute all actions or suits, legal, equitable, or otherwise, which may be in the opinion of the maintenance association, necessary or advisable for the collection of such assessments.

(g) Said assessments shall be subordinate in lien to the lien of any mortgage or mortgages on any property which is subject to such charges regardless of when said mortgage or mortgages were created cr when such charges accrued; provided that such subordination shall apply only to the charges that shall have become payable prior to the passing of title under foreclosure, of such mortgage or mortgages, and the transferee under such foreclosure shall not be liable for payment of any assessment accruing prior to said foreclosure, but nothing herein shall be held to affect the rights herein given to enforce the collection of such charges accruing after sale under foreclosure of such mortgage or mortgages and provided, further, that such charges accruing after sale shall also be subordinate in lien to the lien of any further mortgage or mortgages which are placed on the property subject to such charges, with the intent that no such charges shall at any time be prior in lien to any mortgage or mortgage whatsoever on such property.

7\. Declarant shall incorporate under the laws of the State of Delaware, prior to the conveyance of the first lot hereunder to a homeowner, a non-profit corporation to be known as Hunter's Ridge Maintenance Association, hereinafter referred to as the "Association", for the benefit of all owners, which corporation shall be charged with the duty of improving and maintaining said Private Open Spaces and improvement thereon and Traffic <!-- Page 5 of 14 | printed page -5- | BOOK 815 PAGE 309 --> Islands in good and usable condition, and which shall constitute the maintenance association hereinabove contemplated.

8\. All the record owners shall be entitled to one (1) vote for each lot in which they hold the interest of a record fee, provided, however, that in establishing the Association Declarant may provide for classes of members in which Class A members, being members other than Declarant, may have one (1) vote per lot, and Class B members, being Declarant, may have a greater number of votes, not to exceed three (3) votes per lot. The vote for such lot shall be exercised as the owners thereof may among themselves determine, but in no event shall more than the number of votes be cast with respect to any lot in excess of that to which the class of membership of the owner entitles the owner to cast. Declarant shall be entitled to the number of votes for each lot to which it continues to hold title which the class of its membership entitles it to cast.

9\. The Association shall at no time close or obstruct the Private Open Space and Traffic Islands, except insofar as the same shall be temporarily necessary for maintenance or repairs, or for reasons of safety, provided that nothing herein shall be construed as prohibiting construction or conversion of recreational, maintenance or security improvements, or improvements appurtenant thereto. The Association may adopt rules and regulations concerning the use of the Private Open Space and Traffic Islands for the benefit of all persons entitled to the use thereof.

10\. These covenants and restrictions shall be taken to be real covenants running with the land and binding thereon perpetually.

11\. It is the intention of these declarations that the Association will be an active corporation of the State of Delaware, and in the event that either the Declarant or the maintenance association to be hereinafter formed fail or decline to maintain said Private Open Space and Traffic Islands and improvements as aforesaid, Declarant hereby grants unto New Castle County, its successors or assign, the right, privilege and authority to enter upon said premises and maintain Private Open Space and Traffic Islands and improvements at the expense of the owners of said lots.

12\. In the event that New Castle County elects to maintain the Private Open Space and Traffic Islands or improvements as set forth in paragraph 11 above, all expense of maintenance shall be assessed prorata against the owners of each lot <!-- Page 6 of 14 | printed page -6- | BOOK 815 PAGE 310 --> and shall be collectable by New Castle County in the same manner as provided in paragraph 6 above. The provisions of paragraph 6(g) above notwithstanding, any lien for such expenses of maintenance assessed by the County and filed with the Recorder of Deeds shall be a lien from the time of recording and shall have precedence in relation to other liens, either general or special, including mortgages and other liens, according to the time of recording of such liens in the proper office.

13\. Declarant, for itself, its successors and assigns, grants to the lot owners the free and uninterrupted use of all the Private Open Space and Traffic Islands as shown on the said Plan of Hunter's Ridge in common with the others entitled thereto forever, SUBJECT, nevertheless, to such rules and regulations as the Association may from time to time adopt pursuant to paragraph 9 above.

14\. The following definitions are applicable hereto:

(a) "Association" shall mean and refer to the maintenance association its successors and assigns, and to the proper named corporate entity to be formed as provided hereunder, which Declarant contemplates shall be called "Hunter's Ridge Maintenance Association".

(b) "Lot" shall mean and refer to any plot of land shown on any recorded subdivision plan of the SUBJECT LAND as set forth in Exhibit "A".

(c) "Member" shall mean and refer to every person or entity who holds a membership in the Association.

(d) "Owner" shall mean and refer to the record owner, whether one or more persons or entities, of a fee simple title to any lot which is a part of the real property here set forth.

(e) "Declarant" shall mean and refer to GRANVILLE DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION, a corporation of the State of Delaware, its successors and assigns.

15\. The issuance of Certificates of Occupancy for dwelling units in Hunter's Ridge development shall be proof of the performance of all of the conditions required by this Declaration.

16\. The foregoing covenants shall not be modified amended or altered, in whole or in part except on approval of the <!-- Page 7 of 14 | printed page -7- | BOOK 815 PAGE 311 --> County Council of New Castle County, its successors or assigns, and, following the conveyance of the SUBJECT LAND by Owner to Equitable Owner, the approval of Owner shall not be required for any such modification, amendment or alteration.

17\. The foregoing restrictions shall apply to and bind only the land on the Plan of Hunter's Ridge, and in no event shall the same be construed to apply to or in any way or manner bind or affect any other lands of Declarant, whether such lands are contiguous thereto or otherwise; and no owner of any such lot or lots shall have any right or easements whether in law, equity or otherwise in and to any other lands of Declarant, any law, custom or usage to the contrary notwithstanding.

## Transcription Notes

- p. 1: "289" is printed in numbering-machine style in the left margin beside the title; its meaning is not stated (possibly an instrument or sequence number), and it is not treated as a page number.
- p. 1: The blanks after "recorded" and after "Microfilm No." were left unfilled in the original.
- p. 1: "Declarant are" is kept as printed, including the subject-verb disagreement.
- p. 2: "above-ground" (paragraph 5) falls at a line end ("above-/ground"); the hyphen is kept as intrinsic, but it could also be "aboveground".
- p. 3: "paying fields" is kept as printed; the word clearly reads "paying" and may be an original typo for "playing".
- p. 3: "Private Open Space" is singular on its first appearance and "Private Open Spaces" is plural twice later, and "Association" is capitalized once ("members of the Association, or a majority") and lowercase elsewhere, all as printed.
- p. 4: Apparent typos and grammar slips kept as printed: "created cr when" (for "or") in (g), "said assessment are made" in (e), "improvement thereon" in paragraph 7, and "any mortgage or mortgage whatsoever" at the end of (g).
- p. 5: Kept as printed: "its successors or assign" (singular "assign"), "prorata" as one word, and "record fee".
- p. 6: Commas are missing in the original in "maintenance association its successors" (14(a)) and "modified amended or altered" (paragraph 16), and the closing period in 14(a) follows the closing quotation mark.
- p. 7: Three witness signatures appear in the left column with no printed witness names, one beside each Schlosser line and the third on the same line as "BY:"; Dennis W. Neal signs after "BY:" and Terry D. Neal after "ATTEST:".
- p. 7: The "(SEAL)" labels are printed, but no corporate seal impression is visible even though the text says the corporate seal was affixed.
- p. 8: The second acknowledgment (Dorothy J. Schlosser) reads "his act and deed" as typed.
